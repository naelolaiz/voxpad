      Privacy, built in
      Your export never leaves your device.
      
        
      VoxPad reads your files and transcribes audio in your browser. No audio, chat text, filenames, or transcripts are uploaded to, cached on, or stored on a server. There is no upload endpoint, account, or analytics.
      
        
      Your conversation stays in this tab’s memory. Reloading or closing the page releases that working data; downloads are saved to your device when you choose. Only the speech model and runtime are cached in your browser for reuse.
      
        
      GitHub serves the app, and Hugging Face serves the speech model and runtime. These downloads expose ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, to those hosts. They do not receive your conversation.
      
        
      This is enforced, not only promised: the page’s security policy lets your browser connect to this site and Hugging Face only, and the speech model and runtime are used only if they match the exact versions this app was tested with.
